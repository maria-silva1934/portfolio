E-commerce Product Optimization

Practical Sample Case Study

Optimization of a poorly structured e-commerce product listing to improve clarity, customer trust, search relevance and product information quality.

Original Product

Automatic Pet Water Fountain

Original Title

"Pet Water Fountain Automatic Cat Dog Drinking Fountain USB 2L"

Original Description

"Good water fountain for cats and dogs. It has 2L and USB. Water keeps moving and pets like drinking it. Easy to use and clean. Good quality. Buy now."

Price

€34.99

Manufacturer Information

  • 2 L capacity
  • USB powered
  • Replaceable filtration system
  • Continuous water circulation
  • Low-noise pump
  • Suitable for cats and small dogs
  • Pump can be removed for cleaning
  • Replacement filters sold separately
  • Indoor use only

Analysis

Problems identified

Problem 1

The original title is repetitive.

It uses "Pet" and then repeats the audience with "Cat Dog", making the title unnecessarily cluttered.

Problem 2

The description does not communicate enough useful product information.

Problem 3

"Buy now" creates unnecessary sales pressure.

It may reduce trust rather than helping the customer make an informed decision.

Problem 4

The listing does not clearly communicate that the product is intended for cats and small dogs.

Problem 5

Replacement filters are sold separately.

This is important post-purchase information that should be clearly communicated.

Problem 6

The listing does not explain the recommended filter replacement interval.

Problem 7

Important product information is missing.

Material and detailed cleaning instructions are not included in the listing.

Optimized Listing

My proposed version

Optimized Title

2L Quiet Automatic Pet Water Fountain for Cats & Small Dogs – USB Powered

Optimized Description

Keep fresh water circulating throughout the day with this quiet 2L automatic pet water fountain, designed for cats and small dogs.

With continuous water circulation, a low-noise pump and USB power, the fountain provides a convenient indoor drinking solution while remaining easy to maintain.

Key features:

  • 2L water capacity
  • Suitable for cats and small dogs
  • Low-noise pump
  • Continuous water circulation
  • Replaceable filtration system
  • USB powered
  • Removable pump for easier cleaning
  • Designed for indoor use

Please note:

Replacement filters are sold separately. Filters should be replaced periodically according to the manufacturer's recommendations.

Verification

Information I would verify before publishing

Verification Needed

  • What material is the fountain made from?
  • What is the recommended filter replacement interval?
  • Why is the product specifically limited to cats and small dogs?
  • What are the manufacturer's recommended cleaning instructions?
  • Are any replacement filters included with the initial purchase?

Important Principle

What this exercise demonstrates

Key Principle

Missing product information should be verified with the supplier rather than invented to make the listing appear more complete.
